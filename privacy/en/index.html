Privacy Policy

Draft updated / 草稿更新：2026-09-25

Pre-release draft: AdMob is not yet integrated. Do not submit this as a finalized policy until consent controls, retention details and final privacy disclosures have been verified.

1. Developer and scope

Skyline Shots (寂寞的篮球) is provided by individual developer Dai An (代安). This policy covers the game and its support website. Contact: m18210621018@163.com.

2. Game data on your device

The current game does not require an account. Personal best scores, ball selections and audio preferences are stored locally. The current game code does not upload these saves to a developer server. Device backups may be managed by Apple services you enable.

3. Planned AdMob advertising

The first advertising release is planned to use Google AdMob to show one full-screen interstitial after every six completed rounds, at the results screen. The current project has not integrated the ad SDK; this section describes preparation for that release, not collection by the current build. Google discloses that its ad SDK may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic and performance data for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The final SDK version and configuration must be checked before publication. See AdMob data disclosures and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions and choices

If the advertising release performs tracking as defined by Apple, applicable permission must be obtained before tracking. You can manage it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusing tracking does not mean that all advertising data processing stops. Regional consent and withdrawal controls have not yet been implemented; their actual access path will be documented before the advertising release.

5. Support correspondence

When you email us, your address, message and any attachments you choose to include are processed by the developer and email service provider to respond. Do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Sharing, security and international processing

After ads are enabled, Google will process relevant data according to the advertising configuration. GitHub hosts this website. Providers may process data outside your region; consult their policies for details. The developer must verify applicable safeguards and requirements; this policy does not replace required consent or security measures.

7. Retention, deletion and requests

Local saves remain until app data is deleted. Deleting the app normally removes its local saves; offloading may retain them, and system backups require separate management. Removing the game does not necessarily delete data already handled by ad providers. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve requests and necessary follow-up. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information held by the developer. Ad-provider retention and deletion are subject to Google’s policies; applicable channels will be finalized for the advertising release.

8. Children

This policy does not determine the App Store age rating. Intended audience, ad content ratings and age-related SDK settings must be reviewed before the advertising release. Parents or guardians with concerns about inappropriate processing of a child’s information can contact the developer.

9. Website and policy changes

This website adds no analytics, advertising scripts, third-party fonts or forms. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Changes will be published here with an updated date. Any change requiring renewed consent will be handled under applicable requirements.
